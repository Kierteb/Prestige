Built on Trust

Prestige 4 Cleaners is a professional, family-run cleaning company delivering high-quality domestic and commercial cleaning services across Essex and surrounding areas.

Built on reliability, attention to detail and genuine customer care, we pride ourselves on providing a consistent, high-standard cleaning service that our clients can trust. Whether it's a one-off deep clean or a regular weekly service, our goal is simple: to leave every property spotless and every client satisfied.

We understand that inviting someone into your home or business requires trust — which is why we focus on communication, punctuality and delivering exactly what we promise. We aim to build long-term relationships with our clients, not just complete one-off jobs.

How We Work

Our Approach

At Prestige 4 Cleaners, we don't believe in rushed jobs or cutting corners. We work methodically and professionally — because the details matter.

01

Surfaces Sanitised

Every surface is properly sanitised — not just wiped. We use high-quality, effective cleaning products that remove bacteria and leave surfaces hygienically clean.

02

High-Touch Areas Disinfected

Door handles, light switches, taps, remotes — the areas touched most frequently are properly disinfected to protect your family or your team.

03

Kitchens & Bathrooms Prioritised

Kitchens and bathrooms receive our most thorough attention — eliminating grease, limescale, soap residue and grime to leave them gleaming.

04

Attention to the Small Details

Skirting boards. Plug sockets. Corners. The things others miss are the things we notice. That extra care is what separates a good clean from a Prestige clean.
